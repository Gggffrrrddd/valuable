Refund Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Valuable Premium is purchased through Google Play. Google Play’s payment and refund rules apply.

1. Standard refunds

Request a refund through Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & order history. Google Play decides whether a refund is approved under its poli cy and local consumer law.

2. Cancelling a subscription

Cancelling stops future renewals. It does not automatically refund the current paid period. Access usually continues until the end of that period. Manage subscriptions in Google Play Store → Subscriptions.

3. Accidental purchases

Contact Google Play support as soon as possible. We cannot charge or refund a Google Play payment directly and never receive your card details.

4. Expired or failed purchases

Premium entitlement is validated through RevenueCat. If you paid but Premium is not active, use “Restore purchases” in the app or contact support with your Google Play order number.

5. Account deletion

Deleting your Valuable account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately in Google Play.
