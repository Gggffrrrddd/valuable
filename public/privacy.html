Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what Valuable stores, why we store it, and how you can remove it. Valuable is a focus timer and study-break app.

1. Account data

We use Supabase Auth to create and sign in your account. We store your email address for authentication and account recovery, plus your display name, friend code, avatar character choice, and app preferences such as the Compete tour flag.

2. Focus and study data

  • Focus sessions: start and end time, duration, completion status, and an optional subject tag.
  • Performance data derived from your sessions, such as daily totals, streaks, trends, and milestones.
  • Compete goals: exam date, daily target, progress, dream images you upload, and tracker history.
  • Circle data: friendships, friend requests, and a lightweight presence heartbeat showing whether you are idle, focusing, or paused at the study table.

3. Music and break content

Focus music tracks you add and break-content choices may be stored locally on your device. We do not run an advertising profile based on this activity.

4. Premium and purchases

Premium entitlement status, expiry, and source (Play Store, trial, or founder) are stored on your profile. Purchases are processed by Google Play and RevenueCat. Valuable does not receive or store your card or bank details.

5. Diagnostics

Valuable keeps a small local diagnostics buffer on your device (recent app breadcrumbs and errors) to help debug problems. It is not sent to our servers automatically.

6. Service providers

We use Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage. Google Play and RevenueCat handle subscription purchases and entitlement validation. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Retention and deletion

Your data remains while your account exists. You can delete your account in Settings → Delete account. This removes your auth account, profile, focus sessions, goals, tracker history, friendships, presence, and dream images. You can also request deletion from the public Delete account page.

8. Delete your data

You can request account and data deletion from the public Delete account page. You can also email support@valuable.app from the address linked to your account. We verify ownership before removing data.

9. Security

Data is protected with Supabase row-level security so users can only access their own records, with limited friend-visible data needed for the Circle. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your password private.

10. Children

Valuable is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

11. Contact

For privacy requests or deletion help, email support@valuable.app.
